Will it work on my desktop?

ktiler requires KDE Plasma 6.3 or newer in a Wayland session. Plasma 5 and X11 sessions aren’t supported.

Which releases meet the requirements?

  • Kubuntu 26.04 LTS or newer: 26.04 LTS ships Plasma 6.6 and uses Wayland by default. Kubuntu 24.04 LTS ships Plasma 5.27 and doesn’t meet the requirement.
  • Fedora KDE Plasma Desktop or Kinoite: Use a currently supported release. These are the KDE editions, not Fedora Workstation.
  • KDE neon: Use the fully updated User Edition. Neon updates Plasma independently of its Ubuntu LTS base.
  • openSUSE Leap 16.0 or newer: Select KDE Plasma during installation. Leap 16.0 ships Plasma 6.4 with a Wayland session.
  • Debian 13 or newer: Select KDE Plasma during installation. Debian 13 ships Plasma 6.3.
  • openSUSE Tumbleweed or Arch Linux: Use a fully updated KDE Plasma installation.

Check your system

Search for About this System in System Settings. Look for KDE Plasma Version: 6.3 or newer and Graphics Platform: Wayland.

Or run these commands in a terminal in your desktop session:

grep '^PRETTY_NAME=' /etc/os-release
plasmashell --version
printf 'Session: %s\n' "${XDG_SESSION_TYPE:-unknown}"

The first line identifies your distribution. The other two should show Plasma 6.3 or newer and Session: wayland.

See installation steps or try the browser simulator. The simulator doesn’t require KDE and won’t change your desktop.

Shortcuts

Alt + W/A/S/D
Focus up, left, down, or right.
Alt + Shift + W/A/S/D
Swap with a neighboring window or move to the next display at an edge.
Alt + B/\/-
Switch to Binary, Columns, or Rows.
Alt + Shift + R/E
Rotate windows clockwise or counterclockwise.

Open KWin Scripts in System Settings and click Configure next to ktiler. On the Shortcuts tab, type or paste a binding as text, such as Alt+W. Clear a field to unbind it, then click Apply.

If a saved change doesn’t take effect, open a window’s menu with Alt + F3 and choose ktiler → Reload settings.

Layouts

Binary divides the screen into nested splits. Columns places windows side by side. Rows stacks them. Each display can have its own layout.

Drag the shared edge between two windows to resize the tiles. In Configure ktiler → General, you can choose the starting layout.

Spread Resize

When you resize a tile in Rows or Columns, Spread Resize shares the change among the windows on the other side of the edge. With it off, only the two adjacent tiles change. Binary splits always resize both branches.

Set the default in Configure ktiler → General, or toggle it for the session in the ktiler window submenu.

Pause or remove ktiler

Press Alt + F3 on a normal window and open the ktiler submenu to pause tiling or restore original window positions where possible. To turn it off, untick ktiler in KWin Scripts and click Apply.
