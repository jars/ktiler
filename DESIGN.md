# ktiler website

## Direction: Make Room

The homepage introduces automatic window tiling to Kubuntu and other KDE Plasma
users, then lets them install it or try it in the browser. Use a left-aligned
split layout, the familiar proportions of Noto Sans, and cool slate and paper.
The signature is two stacked, unframed simulator displays that loop through
real keyboard moves. Show each shortcut in a lavender callout over the moving
window. Keep autoplay confined to this preview and skip it for reduced motion.
Both views frame the windows in a slim, matte-black monitor bezel. The bottom
bar holds a layout icon and the display name on the left, plus three decorative
hardware buttons on the right.

Avoid a centered slogan with a gradient or an equal-card feature kit. Keep
“Make room for what’s next.” under the descriptive headline, where it can be
read as a tagline rather than a product definition.

## Tokens and layout

| Role | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Page paper | `#f3f7fb` |
| Surface | `#dce6f0` |
| Ink | `#203247` |
| Action and focus | `#245a87` |

Use Noto Sans with a system fallback, 16px minimum for body copy, and 8px
spacing steps. Give sections room rather than enclosing them in cards. Use
crisp rectangular diagrams and small rounded corners on actionable controls.
Animate tile geometry briefly, without bounce. Keep focus changes and pointer
resizing immediate, and skip geometry transitions for reduced motion.

On mobile, show the headline, tagline, and actions before the demo. Keep
every action keyboard-accessible with visible focus and use real text for
labels. Home, Help, and Simulator share their header and typography; the
simulator retains its own canvas, shortcuts, and tutorial.
