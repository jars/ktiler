# Third-party artwork

## KDE logo

The website simulator’s `kde-icon.png` is the unmodified
[Blue Transparent PNG](https://kde.org/stuff/clipart/logo/kde-logo-blue-transparent-128x128.png)
from the [KDE Press Kit](https://kde.org/stuff/clipart/). KDE distributes the logo
under the [GNU Lesser General Public License](licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt), not ktiler’s
MIT license. The [editable SVG source](licenses/kde-logo-blue-transparent-source.svg)
is included unchanged.

KDE and the K Desktop Environment logo are registered trademarks of KDE e.V.
The logo refers to KDE in the simulator; it is not the ktiler logo and does not
imply KDE endorsement.

## Mechanical keycaps

The website’s shortcut keycaps adapt Peiwen Lu’s
[Pure CSS Happy Hacking Keyboard](https://codepen.io/P233/pen/nqrawW). The
copyright and MIT license notice are preserved in [keycaps.css](keycaps.css).
